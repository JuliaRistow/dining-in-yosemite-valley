Web App for Dining Options in Yosemite Valley

ABOUT

This interactive web app helps Yosemite National Park visitors find upscale restaurants, casual dining options, coffee shops, bars and lounges, grocery stores, and ice cream shops in Yosemite Valley. Users can search for an establishment or area of the park, filter locations by dining type and park area, or select a location from the map or list to view additional information.

HOW TO USE THE APP

Use the search bar to find a specific establishment by name or find establishments in an area of the park. Select the filter button to display specific types of dining locations or park areas. Click a symbol on the map or select a location from the list to zoom to it and view its description, dining category, location, whether it's open seasonally or year-round, typical hours, and a link to view more information.

DATA AND IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Dining locations and their descriptive information were compiled from publicly available sources, including the National Park Service and Yosemite hospitality websites. Hours and seasonal availability may change. Visitors should confirm current information with the establishment or the National Park Service before visiting.

This application was created as a student project and is not an official National Park Service application.
